# Project Oogie 0.2.4

A cheat menu.

## New
- **Cheats page in the settings menu** (GitHub #6). Press F1 (or Back+Start
  on a pad) and open the Cheats page:
  - **Infinite Health:** Jack's health stays full; hits and falls do no harm.
  - **Max Souls:** souls stay at 999, the most the game holds, so everything
    at the Witches' Shop is affordable without grinding.

  Both start Off and work at once, no restart. They are kept in settings.ini
  under [Cheats]. A save made while Max Souls is on keeps the 999 souls.

Thank you to DeliriumGuylol for the idea.

## Tested
- Chapter 1's first fight for 3 minutes with both cheats on: health stayed
  full and souls at 999 the whole time, and Jack never fell. Without the
  cheats he went down within a minute and a half in the same test.
- Six chapters spread through the game with the cheats off: no crashes.

## Known
- Steam Deck: some sounds can still cut out (GitHub #3); a log from 0.2.3 or
  later will help.
